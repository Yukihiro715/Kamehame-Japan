05 / GOOD TO KNOW

YOUR QUESTIONS,
ANSWERED.

Do we need to help with cooking?

No. This concept is a visiting home-cook service, not a cooking class. The host prepares your dinner. Guests are welcome to chat and observe, if they wish.

Can you make our meal vegan or vegetarian?

We will try to match you with a suitable host and confirm recipes and ingredients in advance. Japanese dashi, sauces and garnishes sometimes contain fish, egg, soy or wheat. Strict requirements must be checked individually; we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Does this work at any Airbnb or apartment hotel?

Not automatically. The property must allow visiting cooks, and the kitchen needs suitable equipment and facilities. We'll confirm feasibility before accepting payment.

Is this ready to book now?

Not yet. This is a private concept preview. Hosts, dishes, photos, prices, terms and regional regulatory requirements are still being verified.
