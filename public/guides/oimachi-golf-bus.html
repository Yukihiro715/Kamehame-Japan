in Oimachi.

Save this guide before you travel. Your booking email confirms your travel date, departure time, golf course and rental arrangements.

10 minbefore departure

Please arrive at least 10 minutes early.

The vehicle leaves on time and cannot wait for late arrivals. If you are late, it will depart without you. For example, a 06:50 departure means you must be here by 06:40. All times are Japan time (JST).

01 · Meeting point

Telephone box by Tokyu Livable

Near 1-22-5 Oi, Shinagawa-ku, Tokyo

東京都品川区大井1-22-5付近
東急リバブル前の電話BOX付近・ニッポンレンタカー向かい

Reference photos: GOLFPAQ. Signs may change; follow your booking email and tour staff.

Open meeting area in Google Maps ↗

Wait by the telephone box, opposite Nippon Rent-A-Car. The vehicle may stop nearby; watch for staff.

02 · Getting there

Choose the right exit.

  1. Oimachi Station: Central Exit (中央口)
    Head toward the Hankyu / Ito-Yokado side. Allow about 2 minutes from the exit, plus time inside the station.
  2. Walk past the banks.
    Continue past MUFG and Jonan Shinkin toward the meeting area.
  3. Find the telephone box.
    Look for Tokyu Livable, opposite Nippon Rent-A-Car.

The return drop-off may be at the East Exit. Check your booking email for any changes.

03 · Before you leave

A quick checklist.

  • Check the date and departure time in your KAMEHAME JAPAN booking email.
  • Save your booking email and this guide on your phone.
  • Bring your golf shoes, suitable golf clothing and any equipment not included in your confirmed rental.
  • Check your booking email for lunch inclusions and any extras payable at the course.
  • Look for the tour staff at the meeting point. The shared transfer may be a van or minibus.

This is a shared tour. A dedicated English-speaking guide is not included unless explicitly confirmed in your booking.
